## 🎮 Quick Start

1. **Format Code**: `Ctrl+Shift+P`
2. **Search**: `Ctrl+F`
3. **Console**: `Ctrl+Shift+L`
4. **AI Assistant**: `Ctrl+Shift+A` ⭐ NEW!
5. **Shortcuts**: `Ctrl+Shift+K`
6. **Upload Files**: Click upload icon in sidebar
7. **Create Snapshot**: Click History → Create Snapshot
8. **Insert Snippet**: Click snippet icon in editor
